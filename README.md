📚 Case Studies
🏛 Case Study 1 — COMPAS Algorithm: Racial Bias in Criminal Justice AI
Detail
Info
Industry
Criminal Justice / Government
Company
Northpointe Inc. (equivant)
Year
1998 — 2020s
Issue
Racial bias in recidivism prediction


Key Findings:

COMPAS was used in 40+ U.S. states for bail, sentencing, and parole decisions
Black defendants were flagged as high-risk at 1.9× the rate of White defendants with the same profile
Algorithm accuracy of 65–70% — identical to untrained human volunteers
Race excluded from inputs but entered via proxy variables (zip code, employment, family history)
Triggered the EU AI Act and multiple U.S. state fairness laws

What I Learned:

How proxy discrimination works in ML systems
The mathematical Impossibility Theorem of algorithmic fairness (Chouldechova, 2017)
Ethical analysis using Utilitarian, Kantian, and Rawlsian frameworks
How biased training data amplifies historical inequality at scale


💳 Case Study 2 — Apple Card: Gender Discrimination in Finance AI
Detail
Info
Industry
Finance / Fintech
Companies
Apple Inc. & Goldman Sachs
Year
2019 — Present
Issue
Gender bias in AI credit scoring


Key Findings:

Women received 2–20× lower credit limits than men in same households with equal/better credit scores
Gender excluded from model inputs but encoded through income, credit age, and account type
Goldman Sachs fined $106 million by CFPB in 2022
Low credit limits trigger a cascade: higher utilization → lower FICO → higher borrowing costs → wider wealth gap
EU AI Act (2024) classified credit scoring AI as "high-risk" partly in response to this case

What I Learned:

How historical financial inequality becomes embedded in training data
Legal frameworks: ECOA, CFPB enforcement, NY Banking Law, EU AI Act
Credit cascade mechanism and long-term economic harm of algorithmic bias
Counterfactual fairness testing and adversarial debiasing techniques

Ethical Frameworks Applied
Framework
Application
Utilitarian Ethics
Does the AI maximize overall wellbeing across all groups?
Kantian (Deontological)
Are individuals treated as ends, not data points?
Rawlsian Justice
Does the system benefit the least advantaged?
Feminist Ethics of Care
Does the system account for structural barriers?

Technical Concepts
Proxy Discrimination — protected variables excluded but correlated variables encode the same signal
Algorithmic Fairness Metrics — false positive rate, false negative rate, calibration, demographic parity
The Impossibility Theorem — mathematical proof that all fairness criteria cannot be satisfied simultaneously when base rates differ
Explainable AI (XAI) — SHAP values and LIME for interpretable credit/risk decisions
Adversarial Debiasing — training secondary models to detect and remove demographic inference
Regulatory Knowledge
Equal Credit Opportunity Act (ECOA, 1974)
Consumer Financial Protection Act & CFPB Enforcement
New York DFS AI Supervisory Framework
EU Artificial Intelligence Act (2024) — High-Risk AI Classification
Algorithmic Accountability Act (proposed U.S. federal legislation)
